# ABOUT SECTION

## About KodaDev

KodaDev is a startup-focused software engineering studio specializing in full-stack development, blockchain applications, and modern digital product development.

Founded by Guru Wangchuk, KodaDev was created with a vision to help founders and businesses transform ideas into scalable digital products through modern engineering, clean architecture, and user-focused design.

We work with startups, Web3 companies, agencies, and non-technical founders to build production-ready applications that are fast, scalable, and future-ready.

Our expertise spans across full-stack web development, decentralized applications (dApps), AI-powered systems, smart contract engineering, and MVP development for ambitious startups.
The company combines startup agility with strong engineering practices to deliver modern software experiences that solve real-world problems.

Inspired by real product-building experience across blockchain systems, civic platforms, AI inspection tools, smart infrastructure systems, and enterprise websites, KodaDev focuses on delivering technology that is practical, scalable, and impactful.

# FEATURED PROJECTS SECTION

## Selected Projects

### Student Certificate Validation System Using Blockchain

A blockchain-powered certificate validation system designed to improve transparency, authenticity, and fraud prevention for educational credentials. 

### Himalaya Carbon Exchange

A prototype sovereign carbon market platform connecting Bhutan’s National Carbon Registry with institutional buyers through blockchain-enabled workflows for carbon credit issuance and retirement. 

### Bhutan Developer Network

A centralized platform designed for developers, engineers, and builders in Bhutan to collaborate, build professional identity, and form technical teams within one ecosystem. 

### SiteSense Live

An AI-powered real-time construction inspection platform integrating live camera analysis, automated defect detection, voice interaction, and intelligent reporting workflows. 

### CivicPulse

A modern civic engagement platform enabling citizens to report infrastructure issues through real-time mapping and administrative management dashboards. 

### Blockchain-Based Medicine Verification System

A blockchain verification platform designed to improve medicine authenticity, supply-chain traceability, and pharmaceutical safety. 

### Druk SmartPark

A smart parking analytics and automation system integrating KPI tracking, violation monitoring, predictive insights, and operational analytics. 

### Saidpiece Travels Platform

A digital travel operations platform supporting itinerary management, booking coordination, analytics, and operational optimization for tourism services.
